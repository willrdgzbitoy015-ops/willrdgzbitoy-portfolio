About Me

A little about my background and what I love to build.

I'm Will, a 3rd-year BSIT student from Palompon, Leyte. I learn by breaking things and fixing them, and I build websites and apps that make everyday tasks faster and easier.

My rule is simple: wala nang papel-papel. Fine records on paper? Anong gentle gentle? Ilalabas ko na ang aking app. That's how SmartFine was born: QR scans, instant notices and GCash payments instead of paper.

Fun fact: I can fix your PC and teach you HTML like a lumpia recipe, because everything has to be wrapped right.
